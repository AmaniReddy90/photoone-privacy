# PhotoOne Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

PhotoOne is a photo-library cleaner that works entirely on your device.

**What we collect: nothing.** PhotoOne has no servers, no account system, no
analytics and no advertising. On Android the app does not request internet
permission; on iOS it makes no network requests.

**iCloud Photos.** On iPhone, PhotoOne works on your Photos library through
Apple's PhotoKit. If iCloud Photos is on, Apple's system downloads any image
it needs and syncs deletions to your other Apple devices; PhotoOne itself
makes no connection to iCloud.

**Your photos.** With your permission, PhotoOne reads your photo library to
find similar, duplicate and low-quality photos. Analysis (image fingerprints,
sharpness, faces, text detection) runs on your device. Results are stored only
in the app's private storage on your device and are deleted when you delete
the app. Face analysis measures things like whether eyes are open; it does not
identify who anyone is. Text detection only counts text, it never stores what
the text says.

**Changes to your library.** PhotoOne only deletes photos after you confirm,
using your phone's system dialog; deleted photos go to Recently Deleted
(iOS) or Trash (Android 11+). New photos and videos it creates (Best Take,
Space Saver) are saved to your library as new items, keeping the original's
date, location and favourite status; originals are only removed after you
confirm in the system dialog.

**Sharing.** "Share without location" makes a temporary copy of the photo with
its GPS location removed and hands it to your phone's share sheet. It goes
only where you choose to send it; PhotoOne itself sends nothing.

**Your choices.** Photos you mark "Never suggest", groups you keep, and your
settings are stored only on your device.

**Purchases.** PhotoOne Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play.
Apple or Google process the payment under their own privacy policies;
PhotoOne only learns from your device whether Pro is active. We never see your
payment details.

**Notifications.** If you turn on post-shoot reminders, PhotoOne checks for
new bursts on your device and shows a local notification. Nothing is sent
anywhere.

**Children.** PhotoOne does not collect data from anyone, including children.
