PhotoOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

PhotoOne is a photo-library cleaner. All photo analysis happens on your device, and your photos never leave it.

What we collect: nothing. PhotoOne has no servers, no account system and no analytics. The developer never receives your photos or any information about you.

Optional video ads (free plan only). On the free plan you can choose to watch a short video ad to get extra free cleanups. This is the only time PhotoOne goes online, and only when you tap "Watch a video". Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When a video is requested, Google receives data from your device: your advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow tracking), your IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you have agreed, personalise them. Google's handling of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Your photos and the results of PhotoOne's analysis are never shared with Google or anyone else.

  • Your choices about ads: you never have to watch a video. On iPhone you can refuse tracking when asked, or later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change it any time in PhotoOne › Settings › Ad privacy choices. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • PhotoOne Pro has no ads. Pro users never load the ad system.

iCloud Photos. On iPhone, PhotoOne works on your Photos library through Apple's PhotoKit. If iCloud Photos is on, Apple's system downloads any image it needs and syncs deletions to your other Apple devices; PhotoOne itself makes no connection to iCloud.

Your photos. With your permission, PhotoOne reads your photo library to find similar, duplicate and low-quality photos. Analysis (image fingerprints, sharpness, faces, text detection) runs on your device. Results are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. Face analysis measures things like whether eyes are open; it does not identify who anyone is. Text detection only counts text, it never stores what the text says.

Changes to your library. PhotoOne only deletes photos after you confirm, using your phone's system dialog; deleted photos go to Recently Deleted (iOS) or Trash (Android 11+). New photos and videos it creates (Best Take, Space Saver) are saved to your library as new items, keeping the original's date, location and favourite status; originals are only removed after you confirm in the system dialog.

Sharing. "Share without location" makes a temporary copy of the photo with its GPS location removed and hands it to your phone's share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it; PhotoOne itself sends nothing.

Your choices. Photos you mark "Never suggest", groups you keep, and your settings are stored only on your device.

Purchases. PhotoOne Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process the payment under their own privacy policies; PhotoOne only learns from your device whether Pro is active. We never see your payment details.

Notifications. If you turn on post-shoot reminders, PhotoOne checks for new bursts on your device and shows a local notification. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Children. PhotoOne is not directed at children. The developer does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Contact. PhotoOne is published by Amakat Solutions. Questions about this policy: amakatsolutions@gmail.com
